A split-site multi-gig order lands with one port and the second site is left unprovisioned until someone notices. About three orders a month.

## Options

2. Operations assigns the second port by hand from a weekly report; impact: 2 hours a week, Priya's team; phase: Day one
3. Do nothing; impact: three customers a month get a delayed second site; phase: Day one
